I'm an MS student in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics of the University of São Paulo (IME–USP), advised by Prof. Leliane Nunes de Barros. My research distills large Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models into compact reinforcement-learning policies that are fast and reliable enough to deploy on real robots.

Before my MS, I spent two years at IBM Research Brazil, where I was a lead code contributor on the Granite Trust safety team, working on RAG systems and LoRA adapters for Granite 4 and on GPU workloads at scale. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering, with a depth in machine learning, from UC San Diego.

I like working at every layer of the stack. At home, I built a 6-DoF robotic arm from scratch, from the CAD and actuators to the firmware, and I'm now working on the learned policy that will drive it.
